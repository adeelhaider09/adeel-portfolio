About

Engineering depth, delivery ownership

I am an ecommerce technical lead with 8+ years building, integrating and delivering Shopify, Shopify Plus, BigCommerce and WooCommerce stores for clients across North America, Europe and Asia.

My work sits between engineering and delivery. I translate business requirements into technical specifications and developer-ready tasks, track timelines and dependencies, review code, and stay close enough to the codebase to debug a production issue myself. I have led development teams, set coding standards, and acted as the escalation point when a live store breaks.

On the technical side I work daily with Liquid and Online Store 2.0, the Shopify Admin and Storefront APIs, GraphQL and REST, webhooks, and custom Shopify app development. I also handle Core Web Vitals and page speed, technical SEO and structured data, and GA4 / GTM ecommerce tracking.

I use Claude and Claude Code as a daily part of my development workflow — building features, refactoring, reviewing code and writing automation scripts — which has meaningfully shortened my delivery cycles.
